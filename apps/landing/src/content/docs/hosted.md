Hosted Open Artifacts is the same product, run for you at open-artifacts.app. Sign up there with Google and get an instance at your own address. Solo starts with a free month, then $1/month or $10/year.

## Leave hosted

You can leave whenever you want. Your work is yours.

**1. Take your work with you.** A one-step export is on the way. Until then, ask your agent: "Save the latest version of every artifact I own into a folder on this computer." It reads each one and writes the files.

**2. Stop paying.** Open Settings, then Plan and billing, then Manage billing, and cancel. When your subscription ends, the instance becomes read-only: you can still read everything, and nothing is deleted.

**3. Delete your instance.** Email hello@open-artifacts.com from the owner's address, naming the instance (for example acme.open-artifacts.app). We turn it off right away and delete everything for good 7 days later. Until then, we can bring it back if you change your mind.

**What happens to your address.** You can use it again at once, signed in with the same Google account. Anyone else can take it after a 30-day wait that starts when it's deleted for good.
